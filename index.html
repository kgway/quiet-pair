02 / A HUMAN SECOND LOOK

Similar is not identical.

No more pairs above this floor. You can lower the floor to look again.

Cosine similarity is a ranking signal, not a probability or a guarantee. Review pairs independently: A≈B and B≈C never automatically makes A≈C. The audit records decisions; it does not merge or remove source rows.
